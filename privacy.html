Privacy policy

Margin of Loss publisher is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Margin of Loss, on that channel. Last updated 4 October 2026.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, it uses the owner's own channel: it uploads and edits the channel's own videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports. It does not access other people's accounts, and it does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated reports YouTube Analytics provides to the channel owner.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer in the Windows Credential Manager. Analytics reports are saved as files on that computer to plan future videos. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored tokens are then useless and the owner deletes them from the computer.
