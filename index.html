Margin of Loss publisher

An internal tool of the YouTube channel Margin of Loss.

Margin of Loss retells disasters from the official investigation reports. This tool belongs to the channel's owner and works only on that one channel. With the owner's permission it uploads the channel's own finished videos, sets their title, description, thumbnail, captions and playlists, schedules them, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics so the next videos can improve.

It is not offered to anyone else, and it does not access any other YouTube account or channel.
