Margin of Loss publisher

A private, internal tool of Branislav Stojanovic, the owner of two YouTube channels.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer and works only on his own channels: Margin of Loss, which retells disasters from the official investigation reports, and Inside Seat, which shows famous Second World War and Cold War machines from the crew's seat.

What it does with the YouTube API Services

With the owner's permission it uploads the channel's own finished videos, sets their title, description, thumbnail, captions and playlists, schedules them, posts the channel's own sources comment, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics so the next videos can improve. Every upload is one the owner approved.

It is not offered to anyone else, has no public sign-up, and does not access any other YouTube account or channel.
